# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of `mcp-behave` pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected by mistakes, and learning from the experience.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Critiquing technical ideas and implementations on their merits, with clear reasoning and without personal attacks.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, personal attacks, or sustained disruptive criticism.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language and conduct.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

Strong technical disagreement is welcome. Disagreement about architecture, security assumptions, implementation choices, or project direction is not itself a Code of Conduct violation. Keep criticism focused on the work and engage in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all `mcp-behave` project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, code reviews, and any communication channel officially used by the project. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct. The maintainer will explain the reason when doing so, where appropriate.

## Reporting

To report behavior that you believe violates this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainer privately at **navid72m@gmail.com**. Please include a link or description of the relevant interaction and any context that would help with review. You do not need to resolve the situation yourself before reporting it.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as practical. Information will be shared only as needed to understand and address the report. The maintainer will not retaliate against someone for making a good-faith report. Deliberately false or retaliatory reports are not acceptable.

Please use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), rather than this address or a public issue, to report a security vulnerability.

## Enforcement Guidelines

The maintainer will consider the nature and impact of the behavior when deciding how to respond. Possible responses include:

1. **Correction:** A private, written notice explaining the behavior that is inappropriate and why a change is requested.
2. **Warning:** A warning about the behavior and the consequences of continued conduct.
3. **Temporary restriction:** A temporary restriction from some or all project spaces or interactions.
4. **Permanent restriction:** A permanent ban from project spaces and community interactions.

The response may be adjusted to fit the circumstances. The guidelines are not a guarantee that every report will follow the same sequence.
